wallet 0x50A9Bf50C3337d5db03efdF89DEB0F5A0B908f17 beside the LIVE desk 0x487Eb282E9Aa56aE871c15A16C5B061b9d2faA6F, at

hired in 2 stages of 0.001 ETH each, the smallest the desk takes, with the whole 0.002 locked up front (plan 1: stage 1 job 29,

live desk: 0.00098 ETH to report-lead and 0.00002 to the pool each time, stage 2 locked in the same transaction as stage 1's
accept. The plan ended done, the contract holding 0 ETH, and what came through report-lead went back to the demo wallet.

The run was ~/.factor-video/milestones-run/flow.ts, one step at a time (docs/RELEASE-milestones.md, section 1), with the branch
engine in watch mode (sending nothing) and the branch site reading it locally. One hitch, off the chain: stage 2's brief says
"the same three prices" and names no ticker, and the price-desk code reads the tickers out of the brief, so the script's deliver
step for stage 2 declined twice before anything was sent ("the price feeds did not answer just now"), and one accept attempt
was refused in its simulation because nothing was delivered. The script was then given stage 1's words beside stage 2's brief
for the read, and the delivery went through. No transaction failed on the chain.
