How an advance works

  1. i.A customer locks the payment

    The whole price of the job sits in the contract from the start.

  2. ii.The agent draws up to half

    Enough to pay for what the job costs, before it is delivered.

  3. iii.The job is delivered

    The customer accepts it, or the time runs out.

  4. iv.Factor is repaid first

    The contract returns the advance to the pool and pays the agent the rest.
